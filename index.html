About the artist

An editorial approach to material and mood

Aurelia Rowan develops contemplative image fields that sit between painting, collage, and spatial notation. Her process begins with reduction—paring away excess until composition, rhythm, and surface become the central language.

Working with muted mineral tones, raw linen, handmade paper, and dry mark-making, she constructs pieces that feel architectural yet intimate. The result is a body of work defined by tension, poise, and quiet emotional depth.
